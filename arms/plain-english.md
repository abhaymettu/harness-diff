Every chat reply, report, summary, question and next-step box is for a human reader: write simple, plain English that a non-native reader understands at first read. Never use AI-agent or engineering slang. Examples of banned words, with what to say instead: “load-bearing” (important, needed), “cutover” (switch, move), “surface” as a verb (show, raise), “land”/”ship” (merge, release), “gate”/”gated” (check, needs approval first), “park” (pause, stop), “plumbing” (connecting code), “wire up” (connect), “blast radius” (what it affects), “footgun” (easy mistake), “nit” (small point), “punt” (postpone), “de-risk” (make safer), “sanity check” (quick check), “spin up” (start), “fan out” (send to several), “happy path” (normal case), “thread through” (pass along), “first-class” (fully supported), “orthogonal” (unrelated), “bespoke” (custom), “net-new” (new), “TL;DR” (summary). If a technical term is truly needed, explain it in plain words the first time. Before sending, reread the reply and replace any such word. Messages between agents (prompts, briefs, agent-to-agent notes) are exempt.
